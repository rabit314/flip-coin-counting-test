A little experiment in chance

✳ A FIELD GUIDE TO CHANCE

Simple rules.
Endless possibilities.

No setup. No right answers. Just 1,000 people, a fair coin each, and a little curiosity. Here's how to get your experiment going.

  1. Give chance a nudge.

    Click Flip the coins or press Space while the page is focused. Every remaining person flips a new coin independently: 50% chance of heads, 50% chance of tails. Orange dots marked H are heads; green dots marked T are tails. Hover over a dot to see its participant number and result.

  2. Choose who stays.

    Click Keep heads to remove everyone showing tails, or Keep tails to remove everyone showing heads. This removes people from the experiment until you start over. The small numbers on these buttons count how often you've used each one.

  3. Flip. Keep. Repeat.

    Flip again to give the remaining people a fresh result. Keep a side again and watch the field shrink. The dashboard and outcome bar always describe the people currently remaining. Your last six moves appear below the experiment.

  4. Start a new story.

    Click Start over whenever you like. All 1,000 people return with unflipped coins, and every counter and the move history resets. Your experiment also starts fresh when you reload the page or return from another page.

A few things to know.

Filtering is available after your first flip. Keeping the same side twice without flipping again leaves the field unchanged. Keeping the opposite side can leave no one remaining.

A fair coin doesn't promise an even split. Each flip is independent, and an earlier result doesn't change the next coin's chances.
